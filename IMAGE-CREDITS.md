# Image credits and provenance

Every image the site ships, where it came from, and on what basis it is
published. Kept so the claim on the privacy page ("every photograph is of
our own work") can be checked file by file. Update it whenever a file in
`public/photos` changes.

Last audit: 8 September 2026.

Mál ehf.'s own photographs of its own jobs, received from the client as
WhatsApp batches on 22 August 2026. EXIF and GPS were stripped and the files
re-encoded by `scripts/optimize-photos.py`. Rights: the company's own.

## Drawn, not photographed

The catalogue swatches and the four material samples on the catalogue page
are generated by `src/components/ParquetSwatch.tsx` from a tone value. They
are the site's own artwork.

## Removed on 8 September 2026

Seventeen stock photographs left the site during the legal pass, together
with two foreground cutouts lifted from them and nine catalogue swatches.

Four of the seventeen could be traced. The July 2026 backup still hotlinked
twelve Unsplash photo ids; downloading those and comparing them to the local
files by perceptual hash matched:

| removed file | Unsplash id |
|---|---|
| pano-1-gardabaer-eik.jpg | 1581858726788-75bc0f6a952d |
| pano-3-karsnes-askur.jpg | 1631679706909-1844bbd07221 |
| pano-4-mosfellsbaer-eik.jpg | 1618221195710-dd6b41faaea6 |
| pano-5-grafarvogur-hnota.jpg | 1600494603989-9650cf6ddd3d |

Those four were fine to use (Unsplash Licence, commercial use permitted, no
attribution required). They were removed anyway because the slots they sat
in were captioned as the company's own jobs, which they were not.

The other thirteen (hero-herringbone-sun, hero-herringbone-wide,
before-floor, after-floor, break-room-wide, pano-2-vesturbaer-sildarbein,
service-parket, service-slipun, service-malun, grain-eik, grain-askur,
grain-fura, grain-hnota) matched none of the twelve ids, carried no metadata,
and appear in no backup or commit message. No licence could be established
for them, so they are gone.

The nine `parket-*.jpg` catalogue swatches were composed by
`scripts/build-parquet-swatches.py` from Real Dutch Floor board photographs
fetched from realdutchfloor.com. The sources remain, unshipped, in
`photos-library/parket-source/`. They return to the site the day Planki
Parket ehf. or Real Dutch Floor confirm in writing that the photographs may
be published here; until then the cards are drawn.
